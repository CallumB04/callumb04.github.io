# The community

One of my primary goals for PlayRates is to build a large and thriving community of people that share my love for games and tracking the hobby. Whilst the application is still very early in the scope of what features are to come, I made sure to prioritise implementing the key social functionality in the v1.0 build, to hopefully start getting users interacting with each other, and begin slowly building up the community aspect.

This includes:

- Friends and requesting
- A community forum with game-related threads, replies, and upvoting
- A feed of recent activity from friends
- Notifications on friends and the threads you've interacted with

These basic functionalities are only scratching the surface of what I have planned. I will continue to prioritise the community side of the site, not only to allow users to share the experience I want to create, but also because it becomes a strong marketing tool for attracting users, friends telling friends, and improving retention on the platform so existing users keep coming back.

I believe eventually this will be one of the primary reasons that PlayRates stands out from the other competitors in this space, as any person can track their games and reviews on a spreadsheet, but the social layer is what makes the application something worth coming back to.
