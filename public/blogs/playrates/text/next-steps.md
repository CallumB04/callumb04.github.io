# Next steps

As I've already mentioned, PlayRates is still super early on in the mountain of ideas that I have for it, and I plan to continue developing and maintaining the app, whilst trying to build an active and growing community.

At the time of writing this, PlayRates is at v1.0.3, after pushing some smaller changes and bug fixes since the official launch. However, my roadmap is currently planned out for the foreseeable future, and I am actively building and planning lots of upcoming ideas.

PlayRates currently has a small community of 15 users, which has been slowly growing since the recent launch, with the help of SEO, and a small social media presence. Once I have built out a good amount of upcoming features, and have full confidence that PlayRates is a viable option compared to the competitor platform that has been around for much longer, I plan to push harder on social media, and try to build a thriving community in all the ways possible.
