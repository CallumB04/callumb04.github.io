# What is PlayRates

[PlayRates](https://playrates.app) is a video game tracking, rating and reviewing website I am building. As of today, the platform has a small, growing community of users; a constantly growing library of over 100,000 games - powered by IGDB, and hundreds of game logs and reviews.

I am building PlayRates due to my enjoyment of tracking my hobbies and media I consume. I have always enjoyed keeping track of the games I've played, movies I've watched, and books I've read. However, I felt like none of the video game tracking sites had the UI polish and functionality that I desired. Because of this, I decided to begin building my own, with the goal of creating the best and most feature-rich video game tracking site on the market, alongside a large thriving community that I could be a part of.

My development of PlayRates began at the end of 2024. However, due to my lack of professional development experience at the time, I put the project on hold to focus on my Software Development Apprenticeship, and gain the skills required to build an application ready for live users. After almost 2 years in the industry, maintaining large-scale applications with thousands of live users, I finally decided to return to the project, and in the past 2 months I have refined and built the remaining features, ready for launch.
