# Thrown in at the deep end

Shortly after starting at 10X Managers, a small start-up based in the United Kingdom, I was thrown in at the deep end and challenged massively.

Being a small business where all software was built by the founder, I was immediately the only Software Developer within the company. This initially was a major challenge, as I needed to teach myself brand new technologies, and try to understand how to contribute to a fast-paced professional environment for the first time in my career. However, despite the initial difficulties, I quickly realised the incredible benefits and opportunities that this experience would bring me.

Firstly, the freedom. I was given full access to a large-scale software application, used by thousands of people across live client programmes, a project 100 times bigger than any of the small hobby applications that I had built, and was told to just explore. This was an incredible learning opportunity for me, as I began to understand how large-scale applications were maintained, and how a piece of software can effectively contribute to the wider functions of the business.

Next, the trust. Being the only Software Developer in the company, my boss (the founder of the company) had no choice but to put his trust in me to understand and maintain the application he had built and managed for years. This trust, paired with my drive to learn and develop, gave me the ability to start building live functionalities from day one. This started with a small filter dropdown for an internal admin list, but - within only a couple of months - quickly transitioned into much more.

A few examples of features I built within my first 3 months at the company include:

- A personalised AI Leadership Coach, bespoke to each user.
- A mentoring service, with live direct messaging, and a collection of qualified mentors.
- An impact report creation tool, comparing statistics and progress between the start and end of a client's programme.
