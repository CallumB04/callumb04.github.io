# Where are things now

As of September 2026, I am now finished with my apprenticeship, and was given the opportunity to continue working at 10X Managers as a Software Developer. My responsibilities and freedom have only continued to grow, and I am now working towards a Product Management role - becoming client facing, and exploring how to contribute to the business on a larger scale, and drive revenue through the products I create - which I will balance alongside my existing software development responsibilities.

Currently my primary focus and project is the LeadersLab full-code rebuild. Every feature has been migrated, and I am now testing and refining ahead of a full migration in the new year. This project has also been a massive learning opportunity for myself, working inside of such a large-scale codebase, and being fully in control of all technical and architectural decisions, to prepare for future scale and functionality.

I believe the apprenticeship route is a very effective way to enter the industry. Whether you join a larger organisation and have the opportunity to shadow experienced developers, or a situation like myself where you are dropped into the deep end, an apprenticeship gives you the freedom to learn, adapt, and prepare for your career in the industry.
