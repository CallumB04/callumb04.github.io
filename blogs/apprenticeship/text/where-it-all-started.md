# Where it all started

Growing up, I learnt programming as a hobby, exploring many areas such as web, game, and app development. Following this strong interest, my plan after school was naturally always to go into Software Development.

After finishing my A-Level education, I initially dedicated 2 years to a potential esports career, following some success in the industry during my secondary education. However, after cutting my losses and realising this wasn't viable long term, I finally decided to enter the Software Development industry.

My journey started in August 2024, when I decided to dedicate my entire energy to self-learning how to code on a much higher level than my hobby involved when I was younger; alongside searching for an apprenticeship. I decided on this route over university, as I believed my foundational knowledge and strong interest would allow me to succeed in an environment where I can immediately apply my knowledge and learn from others around me.

Finally, in January 2025, after 5 months of studying hard, building projects, and searching for an opportunity, I found one. This began the start of my Level 4 Software Development Apprenticeship with [QA](https://www.qa.com/apprenticeships/), whilst working in my first professional role at [10X Managers](https://10xmanagers.com).
